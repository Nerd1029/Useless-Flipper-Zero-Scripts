# Useless-Flipper-Zero-Scripts-LOL

A collection of absolutely pointless scripts for your Flipper Zero. These scripts will not hack a mainframe. They will not open a Tesla charging port. They will barely even utilize your screen. They exist purely for the giggles.

## 🚀 Features

* **Zero Utility**: Guaranteed to accomplish absolutely nothing productive.
* **Pure Distraction**: Perfect for wasting Flipper battery life.
* **Maximum Confusion**: Confuse your friends with meaningless terminal outputs.

## 💾 Installation

1. Connect your Flipper Zero to your computer.
2. Open the **qFlipper** application.
3. Navigate to the `badusb` or `badkb` folder.
4. Drag and drop the files from this repo.
5. Disconnect and enjoy the uselessness.
